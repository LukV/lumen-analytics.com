Lumen mark.

A half-disc on a hairline — Lumen, on Substrate.

A filled half-disc sits on the diameter of an outlined one. Read literally: the lit analytical surface above the governed substrate layer. Read graphically: a single primitive that survives 16 px and prints in one colour.

01The mark

primary · aubergine
mono · ink (for print)
on dark · light aubergine

Why a half disc.  The brand name says light; the product story says governed. A solid lumen on an outlined substrate makes both legible in one stroke. The negative half stays visible at 16 px because the diameter is a single horizontal line — the simplest possible thing to render at any size.

Why aubergine.  European, considered, not Silicon Valley. #5B3A52 sits warm enough to live on a paper-tinted background and is far from the neon-cyan of the AI-startup vintage we are avoiding.
